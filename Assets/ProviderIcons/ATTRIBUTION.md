# Provider logo attribution

These provider marks are bundled solely to identify the user's configured services in the menu-bar quota list.

- `claude.svg`, `codex.svg`, `antigravity.svg`, `opencode.svg`, and `github-copilot.svg` are taken from [OmniRouteTray's icon definitions](https://github.com/zoispag/omniroute-tray/blob/master/src/icons.js) (MIT), which credit [@lobehub/icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT).
- `kimi.svg` is copied from [OmniRoute's provider artwork](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/blob/main/public/providers/kimi-logomark-dark.svg), distributed in its MIT-licensed repository.

The shapes and colors remain those of their original icon assets (except replacement of `currentColor` with an equivalent explicit color for reliable native SVG rendering). All associated marks belong to their owners. This independent utility is not endorsed by the providers.

- The macOS **22-pixel status-bar glyph** is from [zoispag/omniroute-tray](https://github.com/zoispag/omniroute-tray/blob/master/src-tauri/icons/tray-icon-22.png) (MIT), rendered as a native template image at 11 points. Its distinct six-spoke silhouette replaces the less legible reduced-size dotted OmniRoute desktop tray graphic.
